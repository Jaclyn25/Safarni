# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Arabic- and English-speaking travelers who are exploring destinations, planning trips, comparing places, and managing practical travel details such as visas and bookings. They need quick, trustworthy guidance that remains easy to scan on desktop and mobile.

## Product Purpose

Safarni brings travel discovery, planning, bookings, profile preferences, visa flows, and a conversational travel concierge into one product. Success means a traveler can move from a broad idea to a useful next step without leaving the product or decoding technical language.

## Brand Personality

Warm, capable, and curious. Safarni should feel like a well-traveled local friend: welcoming and conversational, but careful with facts and clear about the travel scope.

## Anti-references

Avoid robotic disclaimers, repetitive templated replies, over-formal support language, dense walls of text, technical provider details, and generic AI decoration that competes with the travel task.

## Design Principles

1. Guide the next travel decision, not just answer the literal question.
2. Keep boundaries friendly: redirect unrelated questions without sounding punitive.
3. Make every response scannable without hiding or truncating useful detail.
4. Preserve the user's language and conversational context.
5. Treat uncertainty and service failures honestly, using calm recovery steps.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support Arabic RTL and English LTR equally, preserve readable line lengths and contrast, expose meaningful labels for icon-only controls, respect reduced-motion preferences, and keep all chat actions keyboard accessible.
